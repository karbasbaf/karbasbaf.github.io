The Scholar

Craft
as Method

Resonance
Across Scales

Years of hands-on work with musical instruments, investigating, building, and preserving them, led to the creation of the Golden Method, a mathematical approach for optimizing soundhole design. That practical experience revealed the acoustic wisdom already embedded in centuries-old craftsmanship, and became the experiential foundation for everything that followed.

Research later expanded into Persian architectural heritage, where related wave-analysis principles were shown to operate, through different physical mechanisms and at different frequency ranges, within ornamental elements long assumed to be purely decorative. Current work asks whether that same analytical language can be extended toward the electromagnetic and plasma wave phenomena of astrophysical environments.
