Experimental Validation of Helmholtz Resonator Arrays as Zero-Energy Acoustic Treatment for Net-Zero Energy Buildings

Next Research (Elsevier) · Karbasbaf, M.M., Ezaan, G.

An examination of 200 spherical resonators in a purpose-built acoustic chamber, developing a unified mathematical model that aligns Helmholtz resonator theory with experimental results.

From Ornamentation to Acoustics: A Quantitative Analysis of Tong Bori Elements in Persian Architectural Heritage

Digital Applications in Archaeology and Cultural Heritage (Elsevier) · Karbasbaf, M.M., Ezaan, G.

A non-invasive acoustic study of the Ali Qapu Palace Music Hall, finding that decorative Tong Bori elements function as precisely tuned Helmholtz resonators governing reverberation time.
