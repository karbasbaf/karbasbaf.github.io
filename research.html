Acoustic Properties of Ali Qapu Palace, Isfahan: A Computational Study of Historical Persian Architectural Acoustics

Karbasbaf, M. M. ([Year]). Acoustic properties of Ali Qapu Palace, Isfahan: A computational study of historical Persian architectural acoustics. Applied Acoustics, [Volume]([Issue]), [Page Start]–[Page End]. https://doi.org/[10.XXXX/XXXXXXX]

Abstract

This study examines the acoustic behavior of Ali Qapu Palace, a UNESCO World Heritage site in Isfahan, Iran, using computational modeling and on-site measurement to characterize reverberation, resonance, and material absorption within its historical construction. Findings contribute to the growing field of acoustic heritage preservation and inform methods for documenting sound as an intangible dimension of architectural cultural heritage.

Digital Documentation of Acoustic Heritage in Islamic Architecture

Karbasbaf, M. M. ([Year]). Digital documentation of acoustic heritage in Islamic architecture. Digital Applications in Archaeology and Cultural Heritage, [Volume], [Page Start]–[Page End]. https://doi.org/[10.XXXX/XXXXXXX]

Abstract

This paper proposes a digital documentation framework for capturing and preserving the acoustic characteristics of historically significant Islamic architectural sites, combining spatial audio recording with structural and material metadata. The framework is intended to support long-term heritage conservation efforts where acoustic character is at risk of being lost to renovation, decay, or demolition.
